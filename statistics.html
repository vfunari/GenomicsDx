Statistical Analysis

Computational modeling and statistical validation for regulatory evidence

I design validation and harmonization studies to produce results that other laboratories can reproduce.

Harmonization and concordance across laboratories

  • Contributed to the Friends of Cancer Research TMB Harmonization Project: an in silico assessment of TMB variation across diagnostic platforms (Phase I) and the alignment of TMB quantification (Phase II).
  • Co-authored a concordance study among 26 NGS laboratories in the NCI-MATCH clinical trial.
  • Led a national effort to standardize tumor mutation burden for immunotherapy decisions.

Analytical validation design

  • Established a new analytical validation template for NGS-based RNA expression profiling.
  • Launched an MSI qPCR test and evaluated its analytical sensitivity.
  • Trained and validated a random-forest fusion classifier with hyperparameter tuning, F1 scoring and 10-fold cross-validation against orthogonal confirmation by PCR, Sanger sequencing and Archer.
  • Validated an ensemble CNV algorithm against FISH, with chromosome-level accuracy of 93 to 99% for monosomy 7 and trisomy 8 and 12, and 97.95% gene-level concordance.
  • Showed 97.7% SNV and indel concordance, 92.5% fusion concordance and 7 of 7 concordant BCR::ABL1 results (IS above 6.4%) against independent assays for a multimodal heme genomic profiling test.
  • Designed and reported validation studies covering limit of detection, precision, accuracy, positive predictive value and negative predictive value.
  • Measured variant concordance against GIAB benchmark truth sets for NGS pipeline validation.
  • Re-validated a solid tumor NGS assay to include MSI and TMB, and cut solid tumor input requirements by 60%.
  • Reached detection of 5 copies per aliquot on expanded pathogen panels.
  • Used truth-set benchmarking to support New York State approval of an NGS fusion assay.

Evidence for regulators, payers and trials

  • Led design-control inputs and outputs and the validation plans and reports for FDA work.
  • Co-designed and helped manage 12 clinical trials with national and international biopharma partners, from design through assay launch.
  • Led clinical validation of stemness biomarkers to show clinical utility in breast cancer.
  • Defined analytical and clinical validation plans, including MolDX sample requirements, for a laboratory adding Heme NGS.

Computational and quantitative genomics

  • Developed a tumor-only HRD test with CNV, LOH and ploidy determination.
  • Set QC cutoffs and assessed outliers in a 700-sample expression study.
  • Characterized genes through large-scale co-expression analysis and genome-scale expression comparisons.

Statistical methods

  • Assay performance: limit of detection, limit of blank, limit of quantitation, precision (repeatability and reproducibility), accuracy, linearity, PPA and NPA, PPV and NPV, and ROC with AUC.
  • Quality control: Levey-Jennings plots.
  • Modeling: Gaussian and mixture modeling, random forest with hyperparameter tuning, F1 scoring, feature importance, and k-fold and leave-one-out cross-validation.
