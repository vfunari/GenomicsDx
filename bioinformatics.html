Featured: RNA fusion probe design and expression assays

  • Senior author on an algorithm that converts the genomic breakpoints of known fusions into RNA breakpoints and probe sequences.
  • It scores candidate isoforms on database presence, review status, MANE, LRG and CCDS membership, transcript support level, and sequence identity with hg38, then keeps the top-scoring 5′ and 3′ pair.
  • Targeted capture sets for 524 and 1,632 known fusions gave 5- to 20-fold more supporting reads than whole-exome capture of the transcriptome in a 12-fusion SeraSeq control, and 10- to 30-fold more in 10 clinical samples with fusions confirmed by orthogonal methods.
  • Reviewed a newly developed RNA-Seq gene fusion test covering MET exon 14 and EGFRvIII skipping events, and engineered new bioinformatics programs to identify fusion probes.
  • Built a single-cell and ultra-low-input expression pipeline from extraction through library prep, sequencing and analysis.
  • Showed that RNA-seq CCND1 expression from total nucleic acid can stand in for t(11;14) FISH in multiple myeloma, with an AUC of 0.87 and 75 to 81% sensitivity and 97 to 100% specificity depending on the cutoff method.
