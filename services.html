How we work together

Each engagement ends with a defined deliverable, so you know what you are buying before we start. The capability pages describe what I know; this page describes the work itself.

Scientific advisory board leadership

  • I led every scientific advisory board meeting, twice a year, at EmeritusDX and NeoGenomics.
  • I can chair or facilitate one for your company.

Advisory on call

  • CSO-level guidance on regulatory, reimbursement and laboratory strategy, on a retainer.
  • Suited to founders, laboratory directors and investors who need a senior scientific voice without a full-time hire.
  • Ongoing review of laboratory activities that need regular oversight, including laboratory operations and quality control reviews such as Levey-Jennings plots and trend monitoring.

Test launch readiness assessment

  • Gap analysis: the test measured against CAP/CLIA, NYS CLEP and payer requirements, with each gap rated by risk and effort.
  • Technical assessment: assay, sequencing platform and bioinformatics pipeline, including QC metrics, versioning, and data and compute handling.
  • Validation plan: study design, sample numbers, acceptance criteria and statistics for accuracy, precision, limit of detection and reproducibility, or a review of completed validation data against that plan.
  • Clinical utility and evidence: what supports the test’s use, what is missing, and which studies would close the gap for payers.
  • Regulatory and reimbursement pathway: LDT, ASR versus IVD or FDA positioning, and the coverage path (MolDX, ADLT, Z-code timing).
  • Budget review: validation and launch costs, covering samples, reagents, sequencing, staff time and vendor fees, checked against the plan and timeline.
  • Operational readiness: SOP finalization, staff training and vendor timelines.
  • Action plan: prioritized steps with owners, sequence and a launch go/no-go checklist.
  • Delivered as a written report and a working session to walk through it.

NGS validation program design

  • Study design for accuracy, precision, limit of detection and reproducibility, with the statistics worked out.
  • Review of results before they go into a submission or laboratory file.

Regulatory pathway strategy

  • Choosing between LDT, De Novo, PMA and CE-IVD routes.
  • Preparing for FDA or notified-body interactions.

Vendor and partner diligence

  • Contract review and technical assessment of sequencing and bioinformatics vendors.
  • Anti-Kickback and Stark considerations for manufacturer-laboratory arrangements.

Investor and board technical diligence

  • A plain-language assessment of a diagnostics company’s genomics claims, validation evidence and risks, for investors and boards.

Bioinformatics and AI/ML model review

  • An independent look at a pipeline or classifier: cross-validation, orthogonal truth sets, and benchmarking against reference materials such as GIAB.
  • Compliance readiness: HIPAA, data storage, and version control.

How an engagement runs

  • A short scoping call to agree the question, the deliverable and the timeline.
  • Focused work, with a written assessment and a working session to walk through it.
  • Optional follow-on support as a retainer.
